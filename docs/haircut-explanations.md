# Revision-specific design details

The native guide and model review studios now expose “Design details” for the displayed revision. The panel reports actual guide length ranges, the brief's permitted bounds and their recorded origin, reliable available-length observations, growth requirements and the latest edit. Default bounds are labeled defaults; missing, inferred or low-quality available lengths remain uncertain. Synthetic fixtures are explicitly identified.

`HaircutExplanation.build` validates the haircut against its scalp, profile and brief before describing it. The report carries the exact haircut hash. The view loads the report off the main actor, cancels stale presentations and only displays a report matching the shown haircut. This includes unsaved previews and original comparison, rather than always describing the saved selection.

These explanations document the actual constraints, not aesthetic suitability. They do not infer desired maintenance, face measurements, hair properties or reasons why a style would suit someone. They do not establish physical feasibility. Styling assumptions remain represented in the report for later curated presentation; raw internal assumption codes are not shown to consumers.
